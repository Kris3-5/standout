Questions organisers ask

Can I bring the exhibitors I already work with?

Yes. Invite them by email and they can book their usual pitch before you open bookings to everyone else.

When do I get paid?

Stand payments are paid out weekly to your bank account, minus commission. Deposits are paid out when they're received.

Do I have to accept every booking?

No. Set any zone to "apply to book" and nothing is charged until you approve the exhibitor.
